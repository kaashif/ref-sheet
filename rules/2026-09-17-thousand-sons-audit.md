# 2026-09-17 Thousand Sons reference audit

Audited all 12 distinct datasheets represented by the roster against Wahapedia's `wh40k11ed` pages (Faction Pack v1.2). Selected loadouts, stat lines, weapon profiles, keywords, datasheet abilities and the listed attachment/enhancement interactions were compared. This is a roster reference, not an exhaustive replacement for core rules or every possible opponent interaction.

- [Magnus The Red](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/thousand-sons/Magnus-The-Red): Profiles, invulnerable save, damaged bracket, mandatory Warlord, two Rituals/+2 tests and all three selectable powers checked. Time Flux affects friendly Thousand Sons units within 6 inches, including Magnus. Treason selects one enemy at the start of opponent Shooting.

- [Daemon Prince of Tzeentch with Wings](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/thousand-sons/Daemon-Prince-of-Tzeentch-with-Wings): Profiles checked with Eldritch Vortex added once: Dark Blessing S5/D2, strike S9/D4, sweep S7/D2. Hunter applies to all attacks against qualifying units, not merely attacks allocated to a Character model. Healing requires destroying the Character unit. Aetherstride is optional and limited to own Movement; its Sustained D3 and charge restriction expire at turn end.

- [Exalted Sorcerer on Disc of Tzeentch](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/thousand-sons/Exalted-Sorcerer-on-Disc-of-Tzeentch): Default weapons checked; optional khopesh is not selected. Has GRENADES, INFANTRY, MOUNTED and FLY. Greatbow attachment legal. Illusions applies while leading. Binding Tendrils selects one hit Infantry unit after shooting in own Shooting phase, so does not trigger from opponent-turn Overwatch.

- [Sorcerer](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/thousand-sons/Sorcerer): Default weapons checked; optional khopesh is not selected. Has GRENADES. Lethal Hits covers both leader and bodyguard weapons while leading. Twisted Sorceries is per model once/battle, own Shooting or either Fight phase, this model's Psychic weapons only. Rubric ranged wound rerolls apply to the leader; Icon excludes it.

- [Sorcerer In Terminator Armour](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/thousand-sons/Sorcerer-In-Terminator-Armour): Profiles, 4++ and Deep Strike checked. No GRENADES. Lethal Hits applies to the attached unit while leading. Marked by Fate requires visibility from this model, lasts only that own Shooting phase and applies only against its selected enemy. Crystal applies to the whole unit in MY Command phase, then requires ingress in MY next Movement phase, even turn one.

- [Rubric Marines](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/thousand-sons/Rubric-Marines): Profiles and per-model saves checked. Three warpflamers/one Soulreaper retained from user selection. Bringers applies to all models in attached unit for ranged attacks, including the leader. Full wound rerolls require target within an objective's range that the attacking player does not control. Icon excludes Characters; flamers already have native Ignores Cover. No native GRENADES.

- [Scarab Occult Terminators](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/thousand-sons/Scarab-Occult-Terminators): Profiles, Wounds and Leadership checked separately for squad sorcerer and other models. Eight bolters include the squad sorcerer's gun; leader's gun is separate. Two Soulreapers and two Hellfyre racks are legal at ten models. Rites protects the attached unit while it contains a Psyker. No GRENADES.

- [Chaos Spawn](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/thousand-sons/Chaos-Spawn): Movement, Toughness, Wounds, Leadership, OC, weapon, 4+ armour/5++/5+++ checked. Start of each player's Command phase: heal up to three lost wounds on one model; no resurrection. No GRENADES.

- [Sekhetar Robots](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/thousand-sons/Sekhetar-Robots): Default meltagun configuration and all four selected weapons checked. Infiltrators, Stealth, invulnerable save and once/turn Overwatch-or-Heroic-Intervention discount checked. No Psychic weapons or Grand Coven offensive Psychic buffs from merely being near a Psyker. No GRENADES.

- [Tzaangor Enlightened](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/thousand-sons/Tzaangor-Enlightened): Spear profile, Lance/Precision, movement and saves checked. Prophesied Doom rolls only for this unit's models within Engagement Range of the selected enemy after its charge. No GRENADES.

- [Tzaangor Enlightened with Fatecaster Greatbows](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/thousand-sons/Tzaangor-Enlightened-with-Fatecaster-Greatbows): Bow and melee profiles checked. Native bow Lethal Hits does not grant Lethal Hits to the Disc Sorcerer's weapons. Malign Trickery moves the whole attached unit, including leader. No native GRENADES; attached unit has the keyword while Disc remains. Model-specific keyword restrictions still apply.

- [Tzaangors](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/thousand-sons/Tzaangors): Blade profile, Scouts and end-of-opponent-turn reserve rule checked. Brayhorn/banner are optional and absent from supplied roster, so their effects are not applied. No GRENADES.

[Grand Coven and Rituals](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/thousand-sons/): checked three selected enhancements, Kindred choices, four Rituals and six detachment stratagems. Clarified own-phase timings and phase-limited Ritual effects. Psychic weapon enhancements and detachment modifiers do not modify Rituals.

[Core rules](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/core-rules/): checked mixed keywords (02.05.01), abilities in attached units (19.04), Explosives (15.05), and weapon interactions. The PDF includes compact reminders for these. GRENADES is native only to the regular Sorcerer and Disc Sorcerer in this roster; Explosives uses a qualifying model for range/visibility.

All selected profiles matched; corrections were omitted conditions, timing and reminders. The two Sorcerer/Rubric pairs share one set of reference cards, as requested. Prior explicit warpflamer/Soulreaper selections remain in effect because the new export does not specify replacement weapons.

Validation: Typst compilation, PDF page-count/A4 check, and rendered-page visual inspection. Existing unrelated changes in sheet.typ and thousand-sons-reference.pdf are outside this audit.
