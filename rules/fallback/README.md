## Reconciliation rules

1. Points always come from official MFM v1.1. Ignore all Wahapedia points.
2. The three C'tan datasheets always come from the official June 2026
   Necrons Faction Pack.
3. Apply the Faction Pack's Rules Updates section over all fallback text.
   In this list, the direct datasheet override is:
   - Orikan the Diviner: remove `Leader`, add `Support`.
   - Technomancer: remove `Leader`, add `Support`.
4. Apply the July update's returned-Character rule over Protocol of the
   Eternal Revenant: a revived Character returns as its own one-model unit.
5. Apply the July limit of one additional CP per battle round when using
   Imotekh's Grand Strategist.
6. Treat all fallback datasheet wording not expressly confirmed by an
   official 2026 source as provisional, even where it appears unchanged.

## Confirmed MFM v1.1 corrections to fallback points

- Orikan the Diviner: 90, not 80.
- Necron Warriors, 20 models: 190, not 200.
- Canoptek Reanimator: 75, not 70 or the fallback page's displayed value.
- Flayed Ones, 5 models: 55, not 60.

The downloaded Wahapedia pages are evidence for rules text only. They are not
the authority for edition, points, Force Disposition, Detachment Points, or
July balance changes.
