# Reference sheet preferences

- The purpose of these sheets is to prevent forgotten rules and abilities during play. Put reminders where they are used, including on every potentially affected unit or weapon, rather than relying on players to remember a separate source card or key.
- Whenever a phase is mentioned, explicitly identify and highlight whose phase it is: **YOUR**, **THEIR** (the opponent's), or **EITHER PLAYER'S**, as appropriate. Never leave a bare phase name ambiguous. Apply this to triggers, usage windows, effect durations and phase reminders.
- Do not show army points totals or unit/enhancement points costs on reference sheets. Keep stratagem CP costs and other gameplay values.
- Do not show roster quantities in unit headings or weapon labels: use `Plaguebearers`, not `10 Plaguebearers`, and `Plaguesword`, not `10x Plaguesword`. Combine duplicate units into one reference card without listing how many copies are in the army.
- Show weapon attacks per model/weapon; express reminders per model where appropriate rather than giving roster-sized attack totals.
- Spell out `Ignores Cover` instead of abbreviating it as `IC`.
- Show Hazardous in bold, visible green with a light green highlight, keeping the keyword unbroken where it fits.
- Keep an attached leader and bodyguard on separate adjacent cards, with one thick red outline around both cards to show their attachment.
- On Great Unclean One weapon profiles, include a starred, highlighted enemy Toughness -1 reminder for Nurgle's Rot. Key it to the selected enemy and the ability's timing; it is a targeted debuff, not a passive aura or a change to weapon Strength.
- Put every applicable roster aura on each affected unit's card, marked with an asterisk. Put numerical buffs beside the base value in the affected profile cell and Hit rerolls in the Hit cells. Explain source, range, god restrictions and selected modes in the key; do not imply alternative aura modes are simultaneous. Include self-aura effects and defensive/Shadow auras, and do not add Hit rerolls to Torrent attacks.
- For every stat that any ability in the army can modify, show the modified value directly beside the base value in the relevant stat cell, marked with an asterisk and highlighted in bold colour. Weapon modifiers belong in that weapon's profile; defensive or model-profile modifiers belong in the affected model's profile. Include conditional incoming buffs as well as self-buffs and enhancements, and explain their conditions without applying them twice.
- For Hit rerolls, put **RR1\*** (re-roll Hit rolls of 1) or **RR\*** (full Hit rerolls), as appropriate, directly in the affected weapon's BS/WS or Hit box. Render the marker, including its asterisk, in bold colour. Keep Torrent attacks free of Hit-reroll markers.
- Highlight incoming auras on every unit they might affect, including Lord of Change's ranged Strength aura and Be'lakor's selected aura. Show numerical effects in the affected profiles and other effects on the recipient card, with starred bold-colour highlights and clear source/range/eligibility conditions.
- When an ability grants weapon keywords, put those keywords directly in the affected weapon's keyword cell, highlighted in bold colour and marked with an asterisk. For Lord of Change's Master of Magicks, select **Sustained Hits D3** for the sheet and show **Sustained Hits D3\*** on Bolt of Change; state that it is the selected option and identify its phase and duration. Do not imply it affects the rod or other weapons.
- Apply the same recipient-side highlighting to Be'lakor's auras: show selected Shadow Lord Hit rerolls in eligible Hit boxes and highlight the selected defensive or Shadow effect on every potential recipient, including himself where applicable. Keep Wreathed in Shadows, Pall of Despair and Shadow Lord explicitly alternative modes; never imply they operate simultaneously.
- Preserve saved army-list exports verbatim, including their quantities and points; these display preferences apply to the reference sheets.
- Match the established Typst reference-sheet style, compile the PDF, and inspect its rendered layout before delivery.
