Army and Detachment Rules

Cabal of Sorcerers: at the start of your Shooting phase, choose an eligible model and one Ritual not already attempted this turn, then take a Psychic test. Roll 2D6; you may Channel the Warp with +1D6. If Channelled dice contain doubles or triples, the model's unit suffers D3 mortal wounds. Magnus attempts 2 Rituals per turn and adds 2 to tests.
Kindred Sorcery - Command phase: choose one until your next Command phase; each can be chosen only once per battle: Imbued Manifestation (+6" range to ranged Psychic weapons), Psychic Maelstrom (+1 to Wound for Psychic weapons), or Wrath of the Immaterium (Psychic weapons gain Devastating Wounds).

Grand Coven Stratagems

Destined by Fate - 1CP, any phase: after a save is failed for a TS Psyker model, change that attack's Damage to 0.
Arcane Focus - 1CP, Shooting: after a Channelled Psychic test, re-roll every D6 in that test.
Devastating Sorcery - 2CP, Shooting: unselected TS Psyker unit's Psychic weapons gain +9" range and full Hit/Wound re-rolls for the phase.
Psychic Dominion - 1CP, any phase: after enemy selects targets, its Psychic weapons gain Hazardous; your targeted TS unit gets FNP 4+ vs Psychic Attacks.
Egotistical Power - 1CP, Command: one TS Psyker unit gets a chosen Kindred Sorcery ability instead of the army-wide choice until next Command phase.
Desecration of Worlds - 1CP, Command: sticky objective for one TS Psyker unit on an objective you control.
