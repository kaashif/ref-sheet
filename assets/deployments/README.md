These are GDM examples; confirm event-specific terrain and measurements before play. Attribution does not imply endorsement or ownership of the source artwork.

Roster baseline: [Nathaniel Bjorge, Lone Star Open 2026](https://listhammer.info/list/6bd8ec2f9675900b85), five big Imperial Knights and no supporting units. It does not contain a Lancer. The page explicitly labels the Lancer as a hypothetical matchup variation, not the published tournament list.

Rules checked against Wahapedia's `wh40k11ed` core rules, Magnus and Cerastus Knight Lancer datasheets; mission text checked against both sides of [GDM Sabotage](https://gdmissions.app/11th/primary-missions/priority-assets/sabotage). Tactical recommendations are independent suggestions, not rules or guaranteed safe placements.
